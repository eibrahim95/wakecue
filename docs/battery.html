Battery calculator

The defaults are my assumptions, not measurements: measure the real sleep current with a multimeter on the positive wire before trusting the result. The result assumes 80% of the cell's capacity is usable, because the ring needs at least 3.5 V and the chip browns out near the same point.

23 daysbetween charges

The lamp is what drains the battery. Sleeping with a once-a-minute clock redraw is assumed to average 1 mA until you measure it (the DS3231 alone is about 0.2 mA, and each redraw wakes the chip), which would last about 3.7 months. Each 10 minutes of the ring at its 300 mA cap costs about 52 mAh, which is about 2 days of that sleep. At the defaults the lamp needs charging about every 3 weeks; measure the background current (roadmap move 3) and replace the default. For longer life, lower the cap or shorten the time the lamp stays on after the prayer.
